Fast breathing and chest indrawing (respiratory distress)
- Count breaths per minute and compare against age-based fast-breathing thresholds.
- Look for chest indrawing (lower chest pulling in during breathing) and grunting.
- Fast breathing together with chest indrawing indicates severe respiratory illness
  and requires immediate referral.
- Fast breathing alone, without indrawing, may be treated with oral antibiotics per
  local protocol, with close follow-up within 2 days.
