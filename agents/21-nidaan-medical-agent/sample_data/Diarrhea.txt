Diarrhea and dehydration assessment
- Ask about the number of loose stools per day, presence of blood, and ability to drink.
- Check for sunken eyes, a slow skin pinch, and the child's level of alertness.
- Severe dehydration (unable to drink, very sunken eyes, lethargic) requires immediate referral.
- Some dehydration can be managed with oral rehydration solution and close monitoring.
- Mild diarrhea with normal drinking and alertness can usually be managed at home with
  oral rehydration solution and zinc supplementation.
