Fever in children (general guidance)
- Ask about duration of fever and associated symptoms such as rash, ear pain, or joint pain.
- Check for danger signs: stiff neck, bulging fontanelle, repeated vomiting, or lethargy.
- If danger signs are present, classify as urgent and refer to the nearest health facility.
- If fever alone is present without danger signs, advise fluids, rest, and weight-based
  paracetamol dosing, with follow-up in 24-48 hours if fever persists.
